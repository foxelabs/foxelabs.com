## How it trades

Gold Scalpel identifies meaningful highs and lows around price and positions
itself on **both sides** of the range, letting the market pick the direction. It
commits only on a genuine break — then switches immediately to protecting the
trade.

Most trades end at scratch or with a small profit, a few ride a clean breakout
for a real win, and large losses are rare because the stop moves to breakeven
early. You do not need a high win rate; you need the average loss kept tiny.
There is no martingale, grid, or averaging into losers.

## Before you buy

Gold Scalpel is a scalper, so **spread is the single biggest factor** in how it
performs. It runs on any broker, but it is built for **raw-spread or zero-spread
accounts**. On a standard wide-spread account the edge erodes quickly.

The free demo runs in the MT5 Strategy Tester — use it to backtest on your own
gold history first. Live results are tracked publicly on the MQL5 signal linked
above.

> **Trading involves risk.** No strategy wins every trade, and past performance —
> including backtests — does not guarantee future results. Test on a demo account
> before committing real capital, and only risk what you can afford to lose.
