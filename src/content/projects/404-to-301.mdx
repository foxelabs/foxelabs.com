## What it does

404 to 301 catches every "page not found" on your WordPress site and turns it
into something useful — a clean redirect, a logged error, or an alert in your
inbox. Instead of losing visitors (and link equity) to dead URLs, you redirect
them where they should have gone.

It has been maintained since 2014 and runs on over 100,000 active sites.

## Why it exists

Broken links happen — content moves, slugs change, other sites link to the
wrong URL. 404 to 301 makes that recoverable: log what's breaking, redirect it
with the right HTTP status, and know when it's happening.
