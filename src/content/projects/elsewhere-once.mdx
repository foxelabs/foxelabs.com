## What it is

Elsewhere, Once tells one short, true story from somewhere in the world every
day: the town that solved a problem in an unusual way, the animal that was
moved by parachute, the trees that were saved in secret. Each video runs about
a minute and is told in simple English.

## Where the stories live

The videos go out on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X. Every story also has
its own page at [elsewhereonce.com](https://elsewhereonce.com), with the
written version, the sources it came from and notes on what was checked. The
home page maps every story and keeps them as stamps in an album you can filter
by place and field.
