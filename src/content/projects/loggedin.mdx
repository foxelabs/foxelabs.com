## What it does

Loggedin limits how many active sessions a single WordPress account can have at
once. When the limit is reached you decide what happens next — reject the new
login, or end the oldest session automatically.

## Why it exists

Shared accounts and leaked passwords are a real risk. Loggedin gives site owners
a simple, dependable control over concurrent access without a heavyweight
security suite. Maintained since 2016, rated 4.9★.
