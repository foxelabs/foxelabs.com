## Why another journal

Most journals ask you to retype what your broker already knows. That is the reason
almost nobody keeps one for long — the work lands right after a losing trade, which
is exactly when you least want to do it.

TickerLog starts from the account instead. Fills come in on their own, partial
entries and scale-ins are reassembled into one position you can read at a glance,
and what is left for you is the only part a machine cannot do: saying what you were
thinking.

## What it is for

A journal is not a scoreboard. The point is to separate the two things that get
confused after a bad week — what the strategy did, and what you did to the strategy.
TickerLog tags every trade by setup, session, and mistake, so that split shows up in
the data instead of in an argument with yourself.
