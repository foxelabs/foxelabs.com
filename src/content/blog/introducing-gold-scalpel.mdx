Today we're shipping **Gold Scalpel**, the first expert advisor out of Foxe
Labs. It's a fully automated MQL5 EA that scalps gold during the London and
New York sessions, and it was built from a very specific frustration: most
gold EAs are either curve-fit demos or account-blowing grid bots.

## Why gold, why scalping

XAUUSD moves. That volatility is exactly what makes it rewarding for a
disciplined scalping model — and exactly what punishes anything without hard
risk controls. Gold Scalpel leans into the volatility with an ICT/SMC entry
model, but caps every trade with fixed-fractional sizing and a daily drawdown
guard.

## Built for prop firms

If you're running an FTMO, MFF, or 5%ers challenge, the fastest way to fail is
a single oversized loss or a bad news spike. Gold Scalpel ships with presets
that respect those firms' daily and overall drawdown rules out of the box.
