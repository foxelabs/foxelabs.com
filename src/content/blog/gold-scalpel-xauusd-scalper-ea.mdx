## What is Gold Scalpel?

Gold Scalpel is a pre-tuned, fully automated MQL5 expert advisor for **XAUUSD
(gold)** on **MetaTrader 5**. Rather than predicting direction, it identifies
meaningful highs and lows around price, positions itself on **both sides** of the
range, and commits only when price genuinely breaks through — never on a touch or a
near-miss. The moment it's in a trade, the focus switches to protection: the stop
advances to **breakeven** as soon as the trade is onside, then trails the move.

It ships **dialled in at the factory** — the strategy's internals are fixed, and you
adjust only a handful of account-specific settings (about eight in total). There's
nothing to optimise, no dozens of parameters to guess at.

## How the gold scalping strategy works

Every trade follows the same disciplined lifecycle:

1. **Wait for a real move.** No breakout, no trade — the EA stays patient.
2. **Enter on the break.** It's already positioned on both sides, so it catches the
   genuine push when it comes.
3. **Protect quickly.** As soon as the trade is onside, the stop moves to breakeven,
   so a winner rarely turns back into a loser.
4. **Trail the move.** The stop follows price, locking in more of a clean run and
   exiting automatically when momentum fades.
5. **Stand aside.** A short cooldown after each trade avoids re-entering the same
   exhausted move.

That produces an **asymmetric risk profile**: most trades end at scratch or a small
profit, a few ride a clean breakout for a real win, and large losses are rare
because protection kicks in fast. You don't need a high win rate — you need the
**average loss kept tiny**, which is exactly what early breakeven is engineered to
do. There's **no martingale, grid, or averaging** into losers.

## Risk control comes first

A scalper lives and dies by its losers, so Gold Scalpel runs several protections
automatically:

- **Early breakeven & trailing** — the primary safeguard that keeps the average loss
  small.
- **Position cap** — bounds how many trades can be open at once.
- **Post-trade cooldown** — a short pause that prevents over-trading one move.
- **Optional losing-streak cooldown** — pause new entries after a run of losses, then
  resume automatically.
- **Automatic weekend pause** — cancels resting orders and stops new trades into the
  weekend gap.
- **Maximum-lot ceiling** — a hard cap on size, whatever the risk calc suggests.

Position sizing is **percentage-of-equity**: set your risk percentage and the EA
computes the lot, capped by your maximum lot and snapped to your broker's lot step.
A live **on-chart dashboard** shows account, strategy state, and position counts,
refreshed every second — even when the market is closed.
